What Is Food Safety?

Food safety means making sure our food is clean, safe, and healthy to eat so we don't get sick!

🧼

Clean

Wash food and hands before eating

🌡️

Cook

Heat food to the right temperature

❄️

Chill

Keep cold food in the refrigerator

🫧

Wash Your Hands!

The #1 rule of food safety! Always wash your hands with soap and warm water for 20 seconds.

1
Wet your hands with warm water
2
Add soap and scrub everywhere
3
Scrub for 20 seconds (sing "Happy Birthday" twice!)
4
Rinse and dry with a clean towel
🧴
❄️

Keep Food Fresh

🧊

Refrigerator Rules

  • ✦ Keep fridge below 40°F (4°C)
  • ✦ Store raw meat on the bottom shelf
  • ✦ Don't leave the door open too long
🍎

Fruits & Veggies

  • ✦ Wash before eating, not before storing
  • ✦ Check for bruises or mold
  • ✦ Store in separate containers
⏰

The 2-Hour Rule

Don't leave food out at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Bacteria can grow fast!

🔥

Cook It Right

Cooking food at the right temperature kills harmful germs. Here's a quick guide:

🐔

Chicken

165°F

74°C

🥩

Beef

160°F

71°C

🐟

63°C

160°F

71°C

🌡️

⚠️

Spot the Danger!

Use your senses like a detective! 🔍 Here's how to know if food might be unsafe:

👀

Look

  • • Fuzzy mold growing
  • • Strange colors
  • • Slimy texture
👃

Smell

  • • Sour or rotten odor
  • • Smells "off"
  • • Strong bad smell
📅

Check

  • • Expiration date
  • • Broken packaging
  • • Dented cans

🚫 When in doubt, throw it out!
